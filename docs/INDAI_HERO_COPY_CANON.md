## Strategic Rationale

The HERO copy is intentionally:
- neutral and professional,
- free of marketing exaggeration,
- applicable to the full service portfolio (cleaning, hydro-cutting, demolition, cryo-blasting, surface treatment),
- not tied to a specific industry, facility type, or KPI.

The wording reflects a principle of work, not a promise of specific outcomes.

This perception has priority over visual impact, conversion tactics, or promotional messaging.
