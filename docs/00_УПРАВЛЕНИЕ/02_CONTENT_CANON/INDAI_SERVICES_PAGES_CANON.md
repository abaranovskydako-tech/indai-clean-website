CANONICAL PAGE STRUCTURE (7 sections, mandatory order) Every service page body **must** contain exactly these sections in this order: | # | Section heading (Russian) | Purpose | Required | |---|--------------------------|---------|----------| | 1 | # [Название услуги] (H1) + ## Проблема клиента | What pain does the client experience? Symptoms from the operator's perspective. | Yes | | 2 | ## Последствия без очистки | What happens if they don't act? Engineering and economic cascade. | Yes | | 3 | ## Метод INDAI | How INDAI solves this. Method selection logic, not brand names. | Yes | | 4 | ## Почему тест-очистка бесплатна | Explain the UVP mechanism (PRODUCT_COPY §2). | Yes | | 5 | ## Кейсы и цифры | Illustrative cases with engineering parameters. Must include disclaimer. | Yes | | 6 | ## Как организуется работа | Process steps from first contact to result verification. | Yes | | 7 | ## Следующий шаг | Canonical CTAs from frontmatter. | Yes | ### 5.1 Section details #### Section 1: Проблема клиента - Opens with 1–2 paragraphs explaining the equipment context - Contains a bullet list of 4–6 typical symptoms - Written from the client/operator's perspective, not INDAI's - Must relate to operational context, not abstract marketing #### Section 2: Последствия без очистки - 4–6 consequences with engineering and economic reasoning - Bold category labels permitted (e.g., **рост энергопотребления**) - Illustrative percentage ranges permitted in engineering context (e.g., "15–40%") - Must not contain guarantees or promises - Closing sentence: link to cumulative cost over time #### Section 3: Метод INDAI - Method selection criteria (equipment type, contamination, conditions) - List of applicable methods (chemical, hydrodynamic, mechanical, combined) - Safety and compliance statement - Must not contain brand names, model numbers, or specific pressure values as marketing claims - Specific technical parameters permitted only as method description (e.g., "150–1500 бар" for hydrodynamic) #### Section 4: Почему тест-очистка бесплатна - Must contain the UVP mechanism explanation - Must include the canonical closing: «Вы видите результат до оплаты. Результат фиксируется в договоре.» - Must frame test cleaning as engineering necessity, not marketing promotion #### Section 5: Кейсы и цифры - **Must** open with disclaimer: *Примеры из практики, приведены для иллюстрации типовых результатов.* - 2 cases minimum, each with: - Equipment type and context - Contamination description - Measurable parameters (before/after where applicable) - Method used - Time to complete - No client brand names - No commercial data (prices, contract values) #### Section 6: Как организуется работа - 6 numbered steps (standard): 1. Discussion of task and site conditions 2. Site visit — equipment assessment 3. Free test cleaning — result documentation 4. Scope and parameter agreement 5. Execution 6. Result verification against agreed parameters #### Section 7: Следующий шаг - HTML comment referencing CTA contract (frontmatter as source) - Primary CTA displayed first - Secondary CTA displayed second - Labels must match frontmatter cta_primary.text and cta_secondary.text exactly --- ## 6. It requires an addendum to make mandatory. --- ## 7. LANGUAGE AND TONE RULES ### 7.1 Language - Page content: **Russian** (per §4: "Page content (services, copy) → Russian") - Slugs, file names: **English, kebab-case** (per §9) - Frontmatter field names: **English** (per §4) ### 7.2 Tone | Permitted | Forbidden | |-----------|-----------| | Engineering, factual | Marketing persuasion | | Client's operational perspective | "We are the best" perspective | | Facts about method | Promises of result | | "Восстановление параметров" | "Гарантируем эффективность" | | "Бесплатная тест-очистка" (UVP) | "Бесплатная консультация" (lead capture) | ### 7.3 Cleaning industry test > If the text could be placed unchanged on a residential cleaning website — it is wrong for INDAI. --- ## 8. PROHIBITIONS ### 8.1 Content prohibitions - ❌ Guarantees or promises of specific results - ❌ Prices, discounts, promotions (PRODUCT_COPY §2) - ❌ Specific brand names or equipment model numbers as marketing - ❌ Before/after as marketing technique (engineering parameters in cases section are permitted) - ❌ Emotional language, urgency, pressure - ❌ SEO spam or keyword stuffing - ❌ Residential cleaning language or associations - ❌ CTA formulations not in PRODUCT_COPY §7 ### 8.2 Structural prohibitions - ❌ Changing section order - ❌ Removing mandatory sections - ❌ Adding sections without following §6 (optional section rules) - ❌ Hardcoding CTA text in React components (must come from frontmatter) - ❌ Industry-specific landing pages (e.g., "Cleaning for oil & gas") — industry as context is permitted, industry as product is forbidden - ❌ Sub-pages per equipment type within a service ### 8.3 Form/UX prohibitions - ❌ Lead capture forms (name/phone/email) in page body - ❌ Callback widgets - ❌ Exit-intent or scroll-triggered popups on service pages --- ## 9.
