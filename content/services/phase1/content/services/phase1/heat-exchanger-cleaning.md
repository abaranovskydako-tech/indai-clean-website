## Heat Exchanger Cleaning

### Purpose of Service

Heat exchanger cleaning is a comprehensive set of engineering operations aimed at removing operational deposits from heat exchange surfaces of equipment in order to restore its correct working characteristics and ensure stable operation.

The service is applied in cases where, during equipment operation, a reduction in heat exchange efficiency, an increase in hydraulic resistance, or a change in system operating modes is observed.

### Where Service Is Applied

Heat exchanger cleaning is used for equipment utilized in:

- heating and cooling systems
- industrial production technological processes
- energy and engineering systems of buildings
- closed and open heat exchange circuits

The service is applicable to various types of heat exchangers, including plate, shell-and-tube, and other designs, provided technical feasibility of the work.

### Character of Deposits

During equipment operation, heat exchange surfaces may accumulate:

- mineral and salt deposits
- corrosion products
- technological contaminants
- organic and inorganic inclusions

The presence of deposits leads to degradation of heat exchange and changes in the calculated equipment operating parameters.

### Approach to Cleaning

The cleaning method is selected based on:

- type of heat exchange equipment
- character and degree of contamination
- operating conditions
- technical limitations of the facility

Various engineering approaches to cleaning may be applied within the scope of work, in compliance with equipment safety requirements and operating regulations.

### Safety and Regulatory Compliance

Heat exchanger cleaning operations are performed with due regard for:

- industrial safety requirements
- equipment operational documentation
- facility regulations
- conditions of current technological processes

Before performing work, an assessment of equipment condition and access conditions is conducted.

### Expected Result

As a result of cleaning:

- the impact of deposits on heat exchange surfaces is reduced
- equipment working characteristics are restored within operating conditions
- conditions are created for stable continued operation

Actual result depends on equipment condition and character of contamination.

### How Work Is Organized

The process of service execution includes:

1. Preliminary discussion of the task
2. Assessment of equipment condition and work execution conditions
3. Selection of cleaning approach
4. Execution of work
5. Result control within agreed parameters
